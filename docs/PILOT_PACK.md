# DropLive student pilot pack

## For the teacher (read first)
DropLive is a test version of a site where fans back creators' live sessions. **No real money is used.** Pledging is a pretend promise to pay. Students should not enter any card, bank or payment details anywhere.

If any student is under 18, please get your school's and parents' or guardians' permission before they take part. Students only need a name that does not have to be their real one, and an email address. They should never share passwords, phone numbers, home addresses or photos.

## Tester guide (share this with students)
1. Open the link your teacher gives you, on a phone or a computer.
2. Make an account. Use a nickname if you like. Pick Creator or Fan.
3. Work through the tasks below. Write down anything confusing or broken.
4. Remember: nothing here costs money, and nobody will ask you to pay.

## Tasks
Fans (do these):
1. Sign up and confirm your email if asked.
2. Browse Drops. Try search, the category buttons and the sort menu.
3. Open a Drop and say, in your own words, what it is for, its goal and its deadline.
4. Pledge to a Drop. Then withdraw the pledge from My Activity. Pledge again.
5. Check that the page still shows the right state after you refresh and after you sign out and back in.
6. On a phone, try the bottom menu and one form. Is anything cut off or hard to tap?

Creators (do these):
1. Sign up as a Creator and fill in your profile.
2. Create a Drop: pick a category, a goal and a deadline in the future. Edit it once.
3. Ask a friend (a fan account) to pledge. Mark that pledge as paid in your dashboard.
4. Check the funding bar and supporter count update correctly.
5. Try Mark refunded, then cancel a Drop with no paid pledges.
6. Try to cancel a Drop that still has a paid pledge. It should tell you to refund first.

## Bug report (copy and send to your teacher)
- What were you trying to do?
- What did you expect?
- What happened instead? (copy any error words you saw)
- Phone or computer, and browser (Chrome, Safari...)?
- Fan or Creator account?
- Screenshot (hide anything personal).
Never include passwords or personal details.

## Feedback questions (1 = bad, 5 = great)
1. How easy was it to understand what DropLive is for? (1-5)
2. How easy was it to find a Drop you liked? (1-5)
3. Was it clear that no real money is involved? (Yes / No / Unsure)
4. What was the most confusing moment?
5. What one thing would you add or change?
6. Would you use it with real money if it were finished? Why or why not?
